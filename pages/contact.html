Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions regarding applications, payments, and document verification.

How do I track my application status?

Login to your Customer Dashboard and click on "My Applications". You will find real-time status timelines from initial operator review to final document completion.

How are payments processed?

All payments are processed securely via the Razorpay payment gateway. You can pay using UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit/credit cards, or Net Banking. Official receipts are issued immediately.

What happens if a form has an error?

Before any application is finalized on government portals, our operators verify all names, parentage, photos, and signatures. If documents are missing or unclear, we notify you to re-upload them.
